1. OUTPUT
---------
- File name: demo.webm (WebM container, VP9 video, no audio track).
- Picture: 720 x 1280 pixels (portrait, a phone screen), 30 frames per second.
- Length: under 15 seconds. Size: under 1 MB.
- It must play in current Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge, and loop cleanly: the first and the last
  second are still (nothing moving except the game's clock).


2. RECORDING SETUP
------------------
- Browser: Chromium, driven by a script (Playwright or similar), showing https://multiply.by/ with
  no browser frame: only the page.
- Viewport: 360 x 640 CSS pixels at device pixel ratio 2, as a touch phone (so the page shows its
  phone layout and the picture is sharp at 720 x 1280).
- The recording must be sharp: captured at 720 x 1280 real pixels, never 360 x 640 enlarged and
  never the page in one corner of a bigger frame. (Playwright's own video recording ignores the
  device pixel ratio. Chromium's screencast gives 720 x 1280 frames when the browser is started
  with `--force-device-scale-factor=2`.)
- A fresh visitor: no saved progress, no cookies, nothing in storage. The game therefore starts at
  Score 0, Learned 0% and Time 0:00, in the default "2 to 9" mode.
- Record only after the page has fully loaded and drawn. The video must never show a blank or
  half-drawn page: cut everything before that.
- TAP MARKER: a viewer cannot see a finger, so every tap is shown by a marker the recording script
  adds on top of the page (it is not part of the game): a round dot, 44px across, see-through blue
  (rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.35)) with a 2px solid #2563eb border, centred on the tap point. It fades in
  over 150ms BEFORE the tap, and after the tap it grows to 1.4 times its size while fading out over
  300ms. It must not catch the tap itself (`pointer-events: none`) and sits above everything.
- The script must not change the page in any other way: no edited styles, no changed game state, no
  calls to the game's own functions. Every answer is a real tap on a real button.


3. STORYBOARD
-------------
Play the first 4 questions of a new game, each answered right at the first try.
1. Hold the opening screen for 1 second: the first question, four answers, Score 0, Time 0:00.
2. For each of the 4 questions, in turn:
   - Wait about 1.2 seconds with the question on screen (time for a viewer to read it and think of
     the answer).
   - Read the question from the page (for example "7 x 8"), work out the product, and find the
     answer button showing exactly that number.
   - Show the tap marker on the middle of that button, then tap it.
   - The game turns the button green, floats the points up and deals the next question. Wait for
     the next question to be on screen before going on.
3. After the 4th right answer, hold for 1.5 seconds on the 5th question, so the viewer sees the
   result: Score 46 (10 + 11 + 12 + 13), Streak 4, Learned 11%, Mistakes 0. Then the video ends.
