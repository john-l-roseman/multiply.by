-------------
CUSTOMIZATION
-------------
Create a dataset of 25 encouraging short phrases for wrong answers, along with their Chinese translations. Example: “Keep trying!”.

When you build the game, add these visual and sound effects.

Sound effects:
Right Answer: Pop / Bubble Click – A short, satisfying, organic "pop" sound with a bright tone.
Wrong Answer: Select an encouraging phrase, say that phrase in English.

Visual effects:
Right Answer: The cards turn green and melt away.
Wrong Answer: The left card displays the Chinese text of the encouraging phrase at xl size for 2 seconds. 

Enable audio by default.
