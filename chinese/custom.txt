-------------
CUSTOMIZATION
-------------
Create a dataset of 10 encouraging short phrases for wrong answers:
“Keep trying!”
“You can do it!”
“Mistakes help us learn!”
“Don’t give up!”
“Nice effort!”
“You’ll get the next one!”
“Keep going!”
“Every try makes you stronger!”
“That’s how we learn!”
“On to the next one!”

Add a Chinese translation for each phrase to the dataset.

When you build the game, add these visual and sound effects.

Sound effects:
Wrong Answer: Select an encouraging phrase at random, say that phrase in Chinese.

Visual effects:
Right Answer: Flip the question card. It turns over like a flash card to show the next fact. 
It's charming, but a flip needs about 300 ms to read well, so it adds a noticeable pause, and 
fast players may find that annoying.

Wrong Answer: The right answer card switches to green background.
The wrong answer that was selected shows red background.
One of the other wrong answer cards displays the text in Chinese of the encouraging 
phrase that is spoken in the "Sound effects", at xxl size dark grey for 4 seconds.  
After 4 seconds, the next round starts.

Use Chinese translation for the labels in the indicators column.
Translate the Rules into Chinese.

Treat this CUSTOMIZATION section as the primary source, override any conflicting items 
in the specification below it.
