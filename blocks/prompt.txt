Block Craft: a blocky mining and building adventure. Every right answer is one
swing of the pickaxe or one block placed. First dig down through grass, dirt, stone and
ore to collect blocks, then build with them. Each stage is a bigger build: a hut, a
stone house, a watchtower, a castle.
Original pixel-art style drawn in SVG (square blocks with simple pixel textures),
cracks spreading on the block being mined, blocks popping into the inventory, then
flying into place.
