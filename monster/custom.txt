-------------
CUSTOMIZATION
-------------
This is MULTIPLY MONSTER, a version of the game for children of 7 to 9 (2nd and 3rd grade), served
at https://multiply.by/monster/. The player feeds a friendly monster called Munch: every right
answer flies into Munch's mouth. It is the same game as the rest of this file (the deck, learning,
missed questions coming back, saved progress, sounds, PRACTICE HARD ONES, the site frame) with the
changes below: a monster, a path to walk instead of a progress bar, a sticker book, gentle
mistakes with a dot picture, stars instead of a clock, two easier sets, brighter colors and bigger
celebrations. Everything is drawn with inline SVG, CSS and emoji: still ONE file, nothing loaded
from any server. This game has no size limit, but keep it lean.

CELEBRATIONS NEVER SLOW THE GAME (CRITICAL): every animation below runs alongside the game. The next
question still comes 200ms after a right answer, as before; nothing waits for an animation to
finish. Every element an animation adds has `pointer-events: none` and is removed when it is done.
Animate only `transform` and `opacity`. With REDUCED_MOTION, none of the animations below play
(the monster just changes face, the path just fills).

SETS (replaces the three modes)
- Add these 9 facts to the dataset, after the 120, with ids 121 to 129 (the smaller factor first):
  2 x 10, 3 x 10, 4 x 10, 5 x 10, 6 x 10, 7 x 10, 8 x 10, 9 x 10, 10 x 10
- Two sets instead of three, with MODE 9 or 5:
  - 2 to 9 (MODE 9, the default, https://multiply.by/monster/): every fact whose two factors are
    both 9 or less (36 facts), exactly as before.
  - "2, 5 and 10" (MODE 5, address argument `?easy`): every fact whose factors are both 10 or less
    and include a 2, a 5 or a 10 (24 facts: 2 x 2 to 2 x 10, 3 x 5 to 5 x 10, 3 x 10 to 10 x 10).
  `applyMode(params.has('easy') ? 5 : 9)` on page load. GAME_NAME is CONFIG.GAME for 9 and
  CONFIG.GAME + '5' for 5, so each set keeps its own progress. switchMode's
  `history.replaceState` uses `'?easy'` for 5 and the bare path for 9.
- The names of the sets, wherever they are shown: "2, 5 and 10" and "2 to 9". In How to Play the
  Tables choice has two buttons, data-mode="5" ("2, 5, 10") and data-mode="9" ("2–9").
- Wrong answers for the facts with a 10 follow the SAME LAST DIGIT rule (a factor is 10 or more):
  all end in 0.

SCREEN LAYOUT (replaces the top row and the indicators)
- The game area has a soft sky background: `background: linear-gradient(#e0f2fe, #fef9c3);`.
  The game area, the panels and the "You Won!" overlay use a rounded font:
  `font-family: ui-rounded, "Arial Rounded MT Bold", "Nunito", "Varela Round", system-ui, sans-serif;`
  (the site frame keeps its own font).
- From top to bottom the game area holds: the TOP ROW, the PATH, then the answers grid.
- TOP ROW (`flex: 0 0 34%`): the question card on the left (`flex: 1`) and the MONSTER BOX on the
  right (`flex: 0 0 40%`), with a 0.75rem gap. There is NO progress bar (the PATH replaces it).
- MONSTER BOX: a flex column, centered. On top, Munch (the SVG below), as large as fits
  (`flex: 1; min-height: 0; width: 100%;`, the SVG keeps its shape); under it the points.
- POINTS: the Score indicator is the ONLY indicator shown, under Munch: a small label "Points"
  (0.75rem, uppercase, #6b7280) and the number (`clamp(1.2rem, 6vmin, 1.8rem)`, bold, #111827),
  centered. Keep the other four indicator blocks (#ind-streak, #ind-learned, #ind-mistakes,
  #ind-time) in the page with `display: none`, so `update()` works unchanged.
- FLOATING POINTS ("+13") start just RIGHT of the Points number, not over it: the Points block is
  `position: relative` and each float is `position: absolute; left: 100%; margin-left: 0.25rem`,
  vertically centered on the number, `white-space: nowrap`, `pointer-events: none`. It rises only
  1rem while fading out (800ms), so it never covers the "Points" label, the number or Munch, even
  when several are on screen at once.
- Short screens (`max-height: 500px`): the top row is 40% high, the path 32px.
- Answer buttons: rounded (`border-radius: 1rem`), bold, with a "pressable" look: a 4px solid
  bottom border in a darker shade. Each place has its own color, the same for every question:
  place 0 #fde68a (border #f59e0b), place 1 #bbf7d0 (#22c55e), place 2 #bfdbfe (#3b82f6),
  place 3 #fbcfe8 (#ec4899). Text #111827. The right-answer green and wrong-answer red of FEEDBACK
  replace these colors while they show. The question card: white, `border-radius: 1rem`, the same
  4px bottom border in #d1d5db.

MUNCH (the monster, one inline <svg id="munch" viewBox="0 0 120 120">)
- A round, friendly monster: a purple body (#8b5cf6, a wide rounded blob filling most of the
  box), a lighter belly (#c4b5fd), two small orange horns (#f59e0b), two short feet, and two big
  white eyes with dark pupils (#111827) that look a little to the left, toward the question. A
  wide smiling mouth (dark #4c1d95). Nothing scary: no teeth except two small square white ones.
- Parts are separate SVG groups with ids, so faces can change by classes on #munch:
  - IDLE: smiling. Every 4 to 6 seconds (random), it BLINKS: the eyelids (purple ellipses over the
    eyes, normally `transform: scaleY(0)`) close and open in 150ms.
  - MUNCH (a right answer): the mouth becomes a big open oval (#4c1d95, with a pink tongue
    #f472b6) for 250ms, then closes back to the smile, and Munch does a little bounce
    (`translateY(-8px)` and back, 300ms).
  - PUZZLED (a wrong answer): the eyebrows (two short dark lines, normally hidden) tilt in, the
    mouth turns to a small round "o", Munch tilts its head (`rotate(-8deg)` and back, 600ms), and a
    small white thought bubble with a "?" appears above it for 1.5s. Puzzled, never sad or angry.
  - Growth: Munch grows a little with every finished stage of the PATH:
    `transform: scale(1 + 0.06 * stagesDone)` (at most 1.18), `transform-origin: bottom center`,
    with a 400ms transition.
- FEEDING (each right answer, in `handleAnswer`, alongside POP and FLOATING POINTS): a copy of the
  tapped answer's number (a <span> with `position: fixed`, the button's font size, bold, #111827,
  `z-index: 60`) starts at the center of the tapped button and flies to Munch's mouth in 350ms
  (`transform: translate(dx, dy) scale(0.3)`, `opacity` 1 then 0 in the last 100ms, ease-in); as
  it arrives, Munch plays MUNCH. A WORD pops above Munch: one of "Yum!", "Yes!", "Super!", "Wow!",
  "Great!" at random (bold 1.1rem, #7c3aed, white rounded bubble), growing from 0.6 to 1 and
  fading out over 800ms.
- CONFETTI (each right answer): 14 small pieces (8 x 5px rectangles, colors in turn #f59e0b,
  #22c55e, #3b82f6, #ec4899, #8b5cf6) burst out from Munch's mouth in all directions, 50 to 110px,
  spinning, fading out over 700ms. Remove them after 800ms.
- SOUND: after the right-answer chime, a soft "nom": add to SOUNDS
  `nom: ['triangle', [[523, 0, 0.06], [392, 0.08, 0.1]], 0.25]` and play it 300ms after the chime
  (when the number reaches the mouth). Sound now plays at four moments: the chime, the nom, the
  wrong sound and the win tune.

THE PATH (replaces the PROGRESS BAR; always visible, also before the first answer)
- A row between the top row and the answers (`flex: 0 0 40px`), showing one STAGE of the walk:
  up to 12 round STONES in a gently waving line (alternate stones 4px up and down), then the
  stage's LANDMARK at the right end, as a large emoji (1.6rem): stage 1 🏰, stage 2 🌋, stage 3 🌙
  (2 to 9 has 3 stages of 12 facts; "2, 5 and 10" has 2 stages of 12).
- Learned facts walk the path: with L facts learned, the stage shown is `Math.min(Math.floor(L /
  12), stages - 1)` and the first L - 12 * stage stones of it are DONE: filled #8b5cf6 (Munch's
  purple), the others white with a 2px #c4b5fd border. A small 🐾 sits on the next stone to fill.
  Stones are `flex: 1`, at most 22px across, with small gaps, so 12 stones and the landmark fit on
  a 360px phone.
- When a stone fills it pops (scale 1 to 1.4 to 1, 300ms). When the 12th stone of a stage fills:
  the landmark jumps (scale 1 to 1.6 to 1, 500ms), a CONFETTI RAIN falls over the whole game area
  (40 pieces from the top edge at random x, falling 300 to 500px while spinning, over 1200ms), Munch
  grows (see Growth), and after 1 second the path shows the next stage, empty. (These are the
  STAGE celebration; the streak celebration of 12, 24, 36 in a row keeps its burst of dots, but its
  streak glow is dropped, the streak being hidden.)
- During a practice the path counts the practice set the same way (one stage when it has 12 or
  fewer facts). `update()` keeps the path in line with `state` at all times.

STICKER BOOK
- Every fact has a sticker: an emoji from this list of 36, by the fact's place in FACTS of the
  current set (the first fact gets the first emoji, and so on):
  🦊 🐼 🐸 🐙 🦄 🐢 🐝 🦋 🐳 🐬 🦁 🐯 🐨 🐵 🐧 🦉 🐞 🦖 🐲 🐰 🍩 🍕 🍓 🍉 🍦 🧁 🍪 🍭 🚀 🌈 🐠 🌙 ⚽ 🎈 🎁 🧸
- The COLLECTION is the list of fact ids ever learned in this set, saved in
  `'openlang_' + CONFIG.TARGET_LANGUAGE + '_' + GAME_NAME + '_stickers'` (a JSON array, read and
  written with safeStorage). It is NOT part of the progress: Play Again and RESET never clear it
  (a child never loses stickers).
- NEW STICKER: when a right answer learns a fact that is not yet in the collection, add it, save,
  and show a toast "New sticker! 🦊" (with that fact's emoji) just above the path: white rounded
  bubble, bold 1rem #7c3aed, sliding up 10px and fading in, staying 1.2s, then fading out.
- STICKERS (the menu item) opens the sticker book panel, built like the How to Play panel (same
  overlay, backdrop, close button, Escape, and while it is open the answers are ignored, as with
  `state.showInfo`; use `state.showStickers`):
  - Heading "My Stickers" and the count "N of T" (T the number of facts in the set).
  - A grid of T cells (6 columns): a collected fact shows its emoji large (1.8rem) and below it the
    fact ("3 x 4", 0.7rem, #374151); a missing fact shows a light gray circle with "?" and the fact
    below it, so the child can see which ones are left. It fits on a 360x640 phone without
    scrolling (2 to 9: 6 rows of 6).
  - Opening it never starts the timer, like RULES.

GENTLE MISTAKES (replaces steps 2 and 5 to 6 of WRONG ANSWER)
- A wrong answer costs NO points: the score stays as it is (it never goes below 0). The streak
  still drops to 0 and the mistake is still counted (`state.mistakes`, used for the stars).
- Munch looks PUZZLED. The tapped button turns red and the right one green, as before, and the
  QUESTION CARD shows a DOT PICTURE of the fact for 2500ms instead of 2000ms (then
  `nextQuestion()`):
  - On top, the fact with its answer, e.g. "3 x 4 = 12" (bold, about half the usual size).
  - Under it a grid of a rows of b dots (a, b the two factors, the smaller one as rows): round dots
    #8b5cf6, sized to fit the card (each dot about 70% of its grid cell; for 10 x 10 they are
    small, and that is fine), appearing row by row (each row fading in 60ms after the one above).
  - `renderQuestion()` puts the normal question back when the next fact is dealt.
- How to Play says so (see below).

STARS INSTEAD OF A CLOCK
- The clock still runs (for the best score and PRACTICE HARD ONES), but the time is never shown:
  no Time indicator, no "Your time" line.
- On "You Won!", a STAR RATING from the mistakes M of the game and the number of facts T:
  3 stars when M <= T / 12, 2 stars when M <= T / 4, otherwise 1 star. Show three stars in a row
  (2.5rem), the earned ones ⭐ and the others as a gray ☆ (#d1d5db), popping in one after the
  other (scale 0 to 1.2 to 1, 250ms each, 200ms apart).
- The "You Won!" overlay is fully OPAQUE: its own background is the game area's sky gradient with no
  transparency (no rgba, no opacity below 1), so nothing of the game behind it (Munch, the path,
  the answers) shows through.
- The "You Won!" overlay, from top to bottom: "You Won!" (as before), a big Munch (the same SVG,
  about 140px tall) wearing a crown (👑, or a small gold SVG crown, on its head) and dancing (a
  wiggle: `rotate(-6deg)` to `rotate(6deg)` and back, 800ms, repeating; with REDUCED_MOTION it
  stands still), the stars, "Points: X" (1.25rem, #374151), "New best score!" or "Best score: B"
  as before, then the buttons Play Again and "Practice hard ones" (as before) and the next-level
  line.
- A FIREWORKS burst behind the overlay's heading when the game is won: three bursts of 16 dots
  each, at different places in the top half, 300ms apart, each dot flying out 60 to 100px and
  fading over 900ms.
- No "Share my results" button and no results card in this game (drop SHARE MY RESULTS entirely;
  the share icon in the title band stays). No mistakes line on the "You Won!" screen.
- NEXT-LEVEL line: in the easy set, <div>Ready for more? Try <a href="?">2 to 9</a></div>
  (switchMode(9)); in 2 to 9, none. There is NO division line in this game.
- "Practice done!" (after a practice) shows Munch with the crown and the stars too, with the
  buttons Play Again and "Practice hard ones" only.

HOW TO PLAY (replaces the four points; the Tables choice and the SOURCE point stay)
  1. "<strong>Feed Munch:</strong> tap the right answer and Munch eats it."
  2. "<strong>Points:</strong> 10 for a right answer, plus 1 for each right answer in a row. A
     wrong answer costs nothing: Munch shows you the answer with dots."
  3. "<strong>Stickers:</strong> every fact you learn gives you a sticker. Tap STICKERS to see
     them all."
  4. "<strong>Win:</strong> walk the whole path. Fewer mistakes, more stars!"
  5. "<strong>Sound:</strong> the speaker button turns sound on and off."

CHECKS for this game (in addition to the checks of the rest of this file, where they still apply)
- Munch is drawn, friendly, and fits in its box next to the question on a 360x640 phone, in
  landscape and on desktop; nothing is cut off and the page never scrolls.
- A right answer: the number flies into Munch's mouth, Munch munches and bounces, a word pops,
  confetti bursts, a stone of the path fills, and the next question still comes after 200ms.
- A wrong answer: no points lost, Munch looks puzzled, the dot picture shows a rows of b dots and
  the right answer for 2.5s, then the next question.
- The path shows 12 stones and the landmark; after 12 learned facts the landmark jumps, confetti
  rains, Munch grows and the next stage starts. Learned facts and stones always agree, also after
  a reload.
- Stickers: a newly learned fact shows "New sticker!"; the book shows collected and missing facts
  and keeps them after Play Again, RESET and a reload.
- "You Won!" shows the crowned, dancing Munch and the right number of stars; no time anywhere.
- "2, 5 and 10" (?easy) has 24 facts including the 10s; 2 to 9 has 36; each keeps its own
  progress and stickers.
- With reduced motion, nothing flies, bounces or rains, and the game still works.
